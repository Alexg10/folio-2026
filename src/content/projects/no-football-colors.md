## Context

No Football Colors is a media about football culture, with its own merch line. They needed a website for both: a place to publish their stories and a store to sell their products. Freelance project, in collaboration with a design agency.

## What I did

- Built the whole custom site from scratch, media and e-commerce, as the sole developer
- Integrated Strapi with custom page management, so the team can publish and manage editorial content on its own
- Integrated the store through the Shopify headless API: product pages, cart and checkout inside the same experience as the media
- Created a scroll-driven GSAP animation as the centerpiece of the About page

## Outcome

Awarded by CSS Design Awards. The client runs the site independently: new articles and products go live without any developer involvement, keeping the media and the store alive.
