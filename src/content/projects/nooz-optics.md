## Context

Nooz Optics is an eyewear brand selling online through a headless Shopify store built with Next.js, with content managed in Contentful. Freelance project.

## What I did

- Upgraded the site from Next.js 13 to 15, migrating from the Pages Router to the App Router
- Built a new editorial section, with content managed in Contentful
- Set up metadata and structured data (microdata) to improve search visibility
- Added the display of glasses specifications on the product pages

## Outcome

A site running on the latest version of Next.js, with a new editorial section and stronger SEO foundations.
