## Context

X-Scape is a startup that builds proofs of concept for other startups. They wanted a showcase site that reflects that energy. Built at Pelo Studio, where I developed showcase sites for startups such as 1st Kind, Autone and Powens, working directly with designers and clients.

## What I did

- Developed the entire front end with Next.js, with content managed in Storyblok
- Created all the advanced animations with GSAP: the site is a near one-page experience where everything happens on scroll
- Integrated the design with a focus on smooth, fluid motion across devices

At Pelo Studio, I also evolved the agency's stack (Next.js, moving from SCSS to Tailwind, Storybook) and built a starter of reusable components connected to Storyblok for its projects.

## Outcome

A motion-driven showcase site where the animations carry the brand's story.
